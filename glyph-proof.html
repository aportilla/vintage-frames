Glyph proof — the shipped faces’ backfill

Every character fonts/add-glyphs.py adds to the embedded faces — the display face’s additions first, then the body face’s, each grouped by provenance. The big glyph is the embedded font rasterizing that codepoint at 12× (what ships), over a realistic line using it at native size. Feedback in pixels still works: “euro: top bar one px longer”.
