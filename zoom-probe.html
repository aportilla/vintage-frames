zoom probe — the signals src/zoom.ts reads

↑ 1-CSS-px stripes. Crisp black/white at every zoom level ⇒ the engine rasterizes at baseline dpr × zoom. Gray ⇒ it quantized the page scale itself — the one assumption src/zoom.ts cannot verify for itself.

Per engine and display, the questions that matter: does devicePixelRatio track zoom · does screen.width move with zoom · do screenX/Y move with zoom · does a resize event fire on zoom · do the stripes stay crisp. Then drag the window between the two displays at 100% and snapshot again.
