DRAGTHING 2.9 — READ ME                                          17 July 1996

Thank you for trying DragThing, the original dock for the Macintosh.

WHAT’S NEW IN 2.9
• Docks can snap to any edge of the screen, and remember where they were.
• Option-dragging an item onto an occupied tile now swaps the two items.
• The Trash can live in a dock. Drag it out of the corner at last.
• Sounds! Docks click, snap and whoosh. Preferences can silence them.

REQUIREMENTS
DragThing requires System 7.0 or later and about 900K of disk space.
It is native on both 68K and Power Macintosh.

INSTALLING
Run the enclosed installer, or drag the DragThing folder onto your hard
disk yourself. Neither touches your System Folder.

REGISTERING
DragThing is shareware: US$25. Registered users get a code that removes
the startup reminder, plus free updates through the whole 2.x series.

KNOWN PROBLEMS
• After Dark’s “Boris” module steals mouse clicks from floating docks.
• RAM Doubler 1.5.2 with virtual memory ON can slow tile dragging badly.
